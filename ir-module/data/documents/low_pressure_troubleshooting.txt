Low Water Pressure Troubleshooting Procedure

Pressure at a zone inlet that stays below 15 psi for more than 30 minutes is treated as a low pressure event.
The duty engineer should first check for closed valves and pump station failures, then look for an upstream leak
by comparing zone flow with the 7-day average. Reservoir level and time of day must also be reviewed, because
evening peak demand can lower pressure without any fault. If a pumping station has tripped, restart it under
supervision and record the fault. A low pressure event that is not resolved within 12 hours must be escalated
to the network engineering manager.
