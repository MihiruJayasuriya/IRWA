Water Quality Monitoring Standards

All regions must maintain chlorine residual levels between 0.2 and 4.0
milligrams per liter at the point of distribution. Turbidity readings
must not exceed 1 nephelometric turbidity unit in treated water leaving
the treatment facility. Any contamination alert, including unexpected
bacterial presence or turbidity spikes above threshold, must trigger an
immediate boil-water notice for the affected zone until two consecutive
clean samples are confirmed by the testing laboratory. Testing should
occur at minimum twice weekly per zone, with results logged and made
available to the public health authority.
