Pipe Maintenance, Inspection and Flushing Schedule

Planned maintenance windows, including hydrant flushing and pipe flushing, must be announced to customers 48
hours in advance and entered in the maintenance calendar. Flushing raises the recorded flow for the affected zone
and temporarily lowers pressure, so operators must check the calendar before treating a consumption spike as a
leak. Trunk mains are inspected every 12 months and isolation valves are exercised twice a year. Any unplanned
shutdown must be reported to the control room immediately and logged as an incident.
