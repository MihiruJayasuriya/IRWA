Regional Drought Response Plan

When reservoir levels fall below 30 percent capacity, the utility must declare
a Stage 1 drought alert. Under Stage 1, residents are asked to voluntarily
reduce outdoor water use such as lawn irrigation and car washing. If reservoir
levels continue to fall below 20 percent, a Stage 2 mandatory restriction is
declared, limiting outdoor watering to two days per week. Below 10 percent
capacity, Stage 3 emergency restrictions apply, banning all non-essential
outdoor water use and requiring industrial users to reduce consumption by 25
percent. Zone-level reservoir data should be reviewed weekly by the operations
team to determine the appropriate drought stage for each region.
