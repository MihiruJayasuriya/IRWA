Emergency Water Supply and Tanker Distribution

When a zone loses its piped supply for more than 6 hours, the utility must activate the emergency supply plan
and deploy water tankers to designated collection points. Hospitals, schools and elderly care homes are served
first. Each resident is entitled to at least 20 liters of drinking water per day during an outage. Residents
without water should be told where the nearest collection point is by SMS and on the public website. Status
updates on the outage and the expected restoration time must be published every 4 hours until normal supply
resumes. Tanker water must be tested for chlorine residual before distribution.
