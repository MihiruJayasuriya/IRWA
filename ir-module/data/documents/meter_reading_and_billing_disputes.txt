Meter Reading and Billing Dispute Policy

Customer meters are read once a month. When a meter cannot be read the bill is estimated and clearly marked as
an estimate, and the customer may ask for a meter test at no cost if they doubt the reading. Any bill that is
more than 200 percent of the customer's six month average is reviewed automatically for an internal leak before
it is issued. If a hidden leak on the customer's property is confirmed, the excess charge is reduced according to
the leak adjustment rules. Billing disputes must be acknowledged within 3 working days and resolved within 30 days.
