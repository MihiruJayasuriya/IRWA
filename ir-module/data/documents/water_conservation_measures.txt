Water Conservation and Demand Management Guidelines

Households are encouraged to fix dripping taps and leaking toilets, install low flow fixtures and water gardens
before 8 am or after 6 pm to reduce evaporation. During periods of high demand the utility runs public awareness
campaigns and may introduce tiered tariffs so that heavy users pay a higher rate. Large industrial and commercial
customers should complete a water audit every two years and set a reduction target. Conservation results are
reviewed each quarter and shared with the public.
