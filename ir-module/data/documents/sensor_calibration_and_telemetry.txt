Sensor Calibration and Telemetry Maintenance

Flow meters and reservoir level sensors must be calibrated every 6 months and checked against a manual gauge
reading. A sensor that reports an identical value for more than 24 hours, or a value outside its physical range,
is treated as a suspected fault. A technician must verify a suspected sensor fault within 48 hours. Readings from
a suspect sensor must not be used for drought staging or billing decisions until the sensor has been verified.
Lost telemetry links should be logged as data gaps rather than filled with estimated values.
