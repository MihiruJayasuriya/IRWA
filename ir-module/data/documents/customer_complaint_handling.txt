Customer Complaint Handling Guidelines

Complaints related to water discoloration or unusual odor should be
escalated to the water quality team within 4 hours. Complaints about
low water pressure should be cross-referenced against the reservoir
levels and pipe flow data for that zone to determine if it is a
localized issue or part of a broader supply shortage. Billing disputes
related to unusually high consumption charges should be checked against
the usage history for possible leak indicators before being processed
by the billing department. All complaints must be acknowledged within
24 hours and resolved or escalated within 5 business days.
