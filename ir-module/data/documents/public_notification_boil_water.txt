Public Notification and Boil Water Advisory Communication

A boil water advisory must be issued within 1 hour of a confirmed contamination alert for the affected zone. The
notice is sent by SMS, local radio and the utility website and must say which streets are affected, that water
should be boiled for at least one minute before drinking, and who to call for help. The advisory stays in force
until two consecutive clean samples are confirmed by the laboratory, after which a lift notice is published by
the same channels. The public health authority must be informed at the same time as residents.
