Leak Detection and Response Procedure

A suspected leak is flagged when flow readings for a zone exceed the
7-day rolling average consumption by more than 40 percent, with no
corresponding increase in rainfall or scheduled maintenance activity.
Field technicians should be dispatched within 24 hours of a confirmed
anomaly to inspect pipe infrastructure in the affected region. Minor
leaks under 5000 liters per day should be logged and scheduled for
routine repair within one week. Major leaks exceeding 5000 liters per
day require immediate isolation of the affected pipe segment and
emergency repair within 24 hours to minimize water loss and prevent
infrastructure damage.
